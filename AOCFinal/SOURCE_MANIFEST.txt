Safety design:
- World access for occlusion remains on the Minecraft client thread.
- Entity decisions are injected at RenderManager.shouldRender.
- TileEntity decisions cover all matching TileEntityRendererDispatcher.render overloads.
- Occlusion is fail-open and bounded per client tick.
- Transparent/non-opaque blocks are not treated as solid occluders.
- Culling affects rendering only; it does not remove entities, unload chunks, or change server/game state.
- Transformer failures are logged and fail open.
